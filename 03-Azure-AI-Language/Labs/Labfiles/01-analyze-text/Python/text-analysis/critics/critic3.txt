9/10 | Flawed, ambitious, and utterly unforgettable
Reviewed by: TheReelCritic

Interstellar is messy, loud, and incredibly ambitious—and I love it for exactly those reasons. In an era of safe, cookie-cutter blockbusters, Nolan swung for the fences. The docking scene alone ("No, it's necessary") is pure cinema, blending heart-pounding tension, perfect editing, and a booming soundtrack into a sequence that literally had me holding my breath. Is the dialogue a little heavy on exposition? Yes. Does Anne Hathaway's character make a frustratingly irrational choice halfway through? Also yes. But the sheer scale of the emotions and the existential dread of time slipping away make it so easy to forgive its flaws. It’s a movie that asks big questions about humanity's place in the universe and dares to answer them with unironic hope.
